Wednesday, Nov. 5th, 2025 Silo Technologies has announced its acquisition of Pet Tiger, a Fresno, California-based agricultural software company specializing in labor and field-cost tracking for growers and packer-shippers. The partnership expands Silo's long-term vision to deliver an integrated, cloud-based platform that unites ERP, payments, and workforce management for the agriculture supply chain.
