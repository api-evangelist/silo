Banneker Partners , a San Francisco-based investment firm, strategically invested in Silo Technologies, Inc. , a leading provider of modern technology solutions for the food supply chain, in October 2024. The partnership between Banneker Partners and Silo is an exciting milestone in Silo’s mission to transform the food supply chain with cutting-edge SaaS solutions.
